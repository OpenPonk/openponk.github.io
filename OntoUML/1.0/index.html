Scope: UFO-A (Endurants & Structural Aspects)

This profile implements the structural ontological foundations of UFO-A:

  • Substance Sortals: Kind, Collective, Quantity
  • Subkinds & Sortals: SubKind, Category
  • Phases & Roles: Phase, Role, PhaseMixin, RoleMixin, Mixin
  • Moments / Intrinsic Aspects: Mode, Quality, Relator
  • Stereotyped Relations: Material, Mediation, Characterization, Derivation, ComponentOf, MemberOf, SubCollectionOf, SubQuantityOf
